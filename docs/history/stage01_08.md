# Stage 01–08: Early reach and conductance studies

**Evidence level:** Historical report; not re-executed during this publication.

The early DDPM studies investigated physical distance, one-evaluation influence,
multi-step reach, accessible evidence and trajectory-level conductance.

The enduring structural point is that reach depends on the graph and update
schedule, not the visual X shape. Dilation can split a grid into disconnected
residue classes. A shell radius alone does not say that every point on that
shell can reach the query. The Stage 4 interpretation of a purely dimensional
transport loss was therefore partially withdrawn in Stage 5.

One-step influence could not distinguish delayed reach from permanent
unreachability. Reach alone also missed remaining estimation headroom. Single-
source and amplitude conductance did not generalize reliably to the half-plane
prior. A later decision-flip feature was exploratory, not a universal law.

**Superseded evidence:** later Stage 9 reconstruction explicitly separated the
R1 measurement used in headroom from the R1 measurement subtracted in gain.
Do not promote the early predictive improvements as independent validation of
a conductance law. Some early checkpoints and dependencies were absent from the
later handoff, so old completion claims are not treated as a verified run here.

The early source reports and aggregate files were available for curation, but
this export does not claim a complete independently reconstructed Stage 1–8
execution history. See Stage 9 for the stronger audit correction.
