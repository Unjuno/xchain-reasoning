# Stage 29: Full-depth controls and cheap correction

**Evidence level:** Historical report; not re-executed during this publication.

Fifteen saved models were tested on 9,600 new half-plane inputs, with repeated
R1/R2/R4/R8 trajectories. The costed primary contradiction-ranked R8 policy did
not significantly beat the full fixed-depth frontier: the reported difference
was about -0.103 percentage points with a confidence interval crossing zero.

A secondary rule that restarted only positive-contradiction inputs worked better,
and shallow R1/R2 reruns were competitive with deeper reruns. Re-averaging the
initial R1 randomness changed some results; that was a secondary same-data
analysis, not a replacement primary trial.

A direct majority-based readout correction reached about 54.003% accuracy versus
52.989% for a shallow contradiction-triggered rerun in the repeated-current
analysis. Thus a benefit from checking evidence did not establish that deep
stateful revision was required.

**Status:** full-depth primary advantage UNCERTAIN. Do not present the
secondary repeated-current result as an independently preregistered PASS.
